# Reducing Turnover in a Call Centre Through Data‑Driven Insights

## Context
Bilal Ahmed was engaged by a BPO client operating a large call centre that was experiencing high employee turnover, especially among its night‑shift agents. The client needed a clear, data‑backed understanding of why agents were leaving and actionable recommendations to improve retention. The project’s goal was to analyse historic HR and operational data, identify patterns of attrition, and present the findings in a format that decision‑makers could easily interpret and act upon.

## What I built
Using Microsoft Power BI as the primary visualisation platform, Bilal extracted raw HR records, shift schedules, and performance metrics from the client’s SQL database and Excel logs. He performed a thorough data cleaning process to ensure consistency across date formats, agent identifiers, and shift codes. Leveraging statistical techniques, he calculated attrition rates for each shift type and applied survival analysis to compare the longevity of night‑shift versus day‑shift agents.

The Power BI dashboard featured:
- An interactive timeline showing monthly attrition trends.
- A drill‑through view that broke down turnover by shift, tenure, and performance score.
- Heat‑maps highlighting the most vulnerable periods (e.g., agents with less than six months tenure on night shifts).
- Predictive insights that estimated the probability of an agent leaving based on shift assignment and key performance indicators.

All visuals were designed for rapid consumption by HR managers and operations leaders, with filters that allowed users to explore specific teams, locations, or time frames.

## Results
The analysis revealed that night‑shift agents left the organisation 2.3 times faster than their day‑shift counterparts—a statistically significant difference that had not been previously quantified. This insight prompted the client to revise its shift policy, introducing a rotational schedule that reduced continuous night‑shift exposure and added targeted support resources for night‑shift staff.

Within three months of implementing the new policy, the client reported a measurable decline in night‑shift attrition, moving the turnover rate closer to the overall centre average. The Power BI dashboard became a staple in monthly HR reviews, enabling ongoing monitoring of retention metrics and fostering a data‑driven culture around workforce management.
