# Inventory Ageing Analysis for an Apparel Brand

## Context
The client, a mid‑size apparel retailer, was struggling to understand why a significant portion of its inventory was lingering on shelves for months without moving. Their existing reports were static spreadsheets that required manual updates and offered little insight into the age profile of stock across different product lines and stores. Management needed a clear, data‑driven view of slow‑moving items so they could take timely action, reduce holding costs, and improve cash flow.

## What I built
I designed an end‑to‑end reporting solution that turned raw transaction logs into a dynamic, interactive dashboard. First, I imported sales and purchase data into a structured workbook, cleaning and consolidating the information to create a single source of truth. Using built‑in calculation capabilities, I derived key metrics such as days‑in‑stock, turnover rate, and gross margin per SKU. I then built a series of visualizations—heat‑maps, bar charts, and slicers—that let users slice the data by product category, store location, and time period. To make the tool user‑friendly, I added automated refresh routines that pull the latest data each morning, eliminating the need for manual copying or re‑entry. The final dashboard lives in a familiar spreadsheet environment, allowing the client’s team to explore the data without learning new software.

## Results
The analysis uncovered roughly **$180,000** worth of slow‑moving stock that had been sitting idle for more than 90 days. Armed with this insight, the merchandising team implemented a targeted markdown plan that cleared **70 %** of the identified inventory within a single quarter, freeing up valuable warehouse space and improving cash flow. The client now has a reliable, self‑service reporting tool that updates automatically, enabling continuous monitoring of inventory health and faster decision‑making across the organization.
