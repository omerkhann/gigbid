# Streamlined Sales Insights for a Pharma Distribution Company

## Context
Bilal Ahmed was engaged by a pharmaceutical distributor facing a critical bottleneck in its sales reporting process. The company relied on manual Excel extracts and ad‑hoc SQL queries to compile monthly performance data for its 40 area managers. This approach required three full days of effort each month, delayed decision‑making, and often resulted in outdated information being presented to the field teams. The client needed a faster, more reliable solution that could deliver up‑to‑date insights with minimal manual effort, while also providing a flexible visual interface for non‑technical users.

## What I built
Leveraging Microsoft Power BI, SQL, and advanced data‑modeling techniques, Bilal designed an end‑to‑end reporting system. He first consolidated the disparate data sources—sales transactions, inventory logs, and regional targets—into a clean, relational model in SQL Server. The model incorporated calculated columns and measures to support key performance indicators such as month‑over‑month growth, product‑level profitability, and forecast accuracy. 

In Power BI, Bilal created a suite of interactive dashboards that allowed area managers to:
- Drill down from national overviews to individual product performance.
- Filter results by territory, time period, or customer segment.
- Visualize trends with dynamic charts and heat maps.

The dashboards were published to the Power BI service, enabling secure, role‑based access and automatic data refreshes on a nightly schedule. Documentation and a brief training session ensured the managers could navigate the reports confidently.

## Results
The new solution slashed the monthly reporting cycle from three days to just two hours—a 96% reduction in processing time. All 40 area managers adopted the dashboards, using them daily to track sales performance, identify under‑performing territories, and adjust tactics in real time. The faster turnaround and richer visual insights led to more proactive decision‑making, contributing to improved sales efficiency across the distribution network.
