# Marketing Funnel Analytics for a D2C Skincare Brand

## Context
Bilal Ahmed was engaged by a fast‑growing direct‑to‑consumer skincare company that sells its products online. The brand relied heavily on digital advertising to drive traffic, but the leadership team noticed that a significant number of visitors abandoned the purchase process before reaching the checkout page. They needed a clear, data‑driven view of the entire customer journey—from the first ad click to the final purchase—to pinpoint where the funnel was leaking and to recommend actionable fixes.

## What I built
Using the client’s web tracking data, I created a comprehensive dashboard that visualized each stage of the marketing funnel. I integrated site‑wide visitor metrics, campaign performance, and user behavior signals into a single reporting view. To capture the exact points where users left the site, I set up event‑capture rules that recorded interactions such as product page views, add‑to‑cart clicks, and checkout initiations. I then layered these events onto the existing traffic data to calculate conversion rates for each step. 

To test potential improvements without disrupting the live site, I implemented a controlled variation system that presented a different version of the checkout page to a small segment of visitors. This allowed the team to compare the performance of the original flow against the modified one in real time. The findings were fed back into the dashboard, where I highlighted the impact of each change with clear visual cues.

## Results
The analysis revealed a sharp drop‑off right after users added items to their carts, indicating a friction point in the checkout experience. By addressing the identified issue—simplifying the form fields and reducing the number of required steps—the brand saw an uplift of 0.6 percentage points in overall conversion. The new dashboard continues to be used by the marketing and product teams to monitor funnel health, run further experiments, and make data‑backed decisions that keep the revenue stream growing.
