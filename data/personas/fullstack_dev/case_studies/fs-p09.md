# Legacy Membership Site Maintenance

## Context
The client, an educational organization, relied on a membership‑driven website that hosted resources for over 4,000 learners. By 2020 the platform was aging and required careful attention to keep it functional during two major version upgrades of its underlying system. The site needed regular content updates, styling tweaks, and assurance that members could continue to log in, access their courses, and receive communications without interruption. The client expected a smooth transition that would not disrupt the learning experience.

## What I built
I took over the site’s day‑to‑day maintenance, focusing on three key areas:

1. **Content and layout management** – I used a visual page‑builder interface to adjust page structures, add new sections, and update existing content without writing code. This allowed the client’s staff to keep the site current while I ensured the underlying templates remained stable.
2. **Styling and responsive design** – By editing style sheets directly, I refined the visual presentation, fixed layout glitches, and made sure the site rendered well on desktops, tablets, and smartphones. All CSS changes were scoped to avoid affecting other parts of the system.
3. **Upgrade coordination** – When the platform released two major version updates, I performed a thorough pre‑upgrade audit, backed up all data, and tested the site in a staging environment. I then applied the updates, resolved compatibility issues, and verified that all membership functionality—login, profile management, and protected content—worked flawlessly.

Throughout the engagement I maintained clear documentation, communicated progress to the client, and provided quick fixes for any emerging bugs.

## Results
The site remained fully operational throughout both upgrades, preserving uninterrupted access for the 4,000+ members. The client reported zero downtime during the transition periods and praised the improved visual consistency across devices. By handling the maintenance in a proactive manner, I helped the organization avoid costly redevelopment while extending the life of their existing investment.
