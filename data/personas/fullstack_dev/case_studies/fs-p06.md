# Marketing Site Redesign for an Accounting Firm

## Context
In 2025, a well‑established accounting firm approached Sara Malik to modernize their online presence. The existing site was built on an outdated stack, lacked responsive behavior, and failed to convert visitors into leads. The firm’s primary goal was to generate more qualified inquiries through the contact form while presenting a professional, trustworthy image that matched their reputation in the professional services sector. Sara conducted a discovery workshop, reviewed analytics, and mapped the user journey to identify friction points. The brief called for a complete visual refresh, mobile‑first design, and a fast, SEO‑friendly implementation using modern web technologies.

## What I built
Sara led the end‑to‑end redesign, starting with high‑fidelity prototypes in **Figma** that captured the firm’s brand guidelines and emphasized clear calls‑to‑action. The development stack was chosen for its performance and scalability: the front end was built with **Next.js** and **React**, enabling server‑side rendering for optimal SEO and lightning‑quick page loads. A responsive layout was crafted using CSS Grid and Flexbox, ensuring seamless experiences across desktops, tablets, and smartphones. Interactive components—such as an animated hero carousel and a dynamic service‑filter—were coded as reusable React components, reducing future maintenance effort. The contact form was integrated with the firm’s CRM via a secure API endpoint, and form validation was handled client‑side for instant feedback. Throughout the project, Sara adhered to best practices for accessibility (WCAG 2.1) and performance (Lighthouse scores > 90).

## Results
After launch, the new site delivered a dramatic uplift in lead generation. The redesigned contact form recorded a **2.4× increase in submissions** during the first quarter, surpassing the client’s target by a wide margin. Page load times dropped by 45 % compared with the legacy site, leading to lower bounce rates and higher engagement metrics. The firm reported improved client perception, citing the modern, responsive design as a key factor in winning new business. Sara’s combination of strategic design, responsive development, and Next.js expertise proved instrumental in turning the website into a high‑performing marketing asset for the accounting firm.
