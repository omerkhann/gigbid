# Internal Admin Dashboard for a Logistics Startup

## Context
In 2023 Sara Malik was engaged by a fast‑growing logistics startup that needed a single source of truth for its operations team. The existing workflow relied on spreadsheets and ad‑hoc scripts, which caused data silos, manual errors, and limited visibility into shipment status. The company wanted a modern, scalable internal tool that could consolidate order tracking, carrier assignments, and performance metrics while allowing rapid iteration as the business evolved. Sara’s full‑stack expertise in React, Next.js, and Django made her an ideal fit to design a unified dashboard that could handle real‑time data and support future feature expansion.

## What I built
Sara architected a clean, component‑driven front‑end using **React** with **TypeScript** for type safety and maintainability. The UI, built on Next.js, offers server‑side rendering for fast initial loads and seamless routing between modules such as Shipments, Carriers, and Analytics. On the back‑end, she leveraged **Django** to expose a RESTful API, handling authentication, business logic, and integration with existing ERP systems. Data is persisted in **PostgreSQL**, with careful indexing to support complex queries on shipment timelines and carrier performance. To ensure consistency across development and production environments, the entire stack was containerized with **Docker**, enabling rapid deployments and easy scaling on the startup’s cloud infrastructure. Throughout the project Sara implemented role‑based access controls, real‑time updates via WebSocket channels, and responsive design principles so the dashboard works equally well on desktop and tablet devices.

## Results
The new admin dashboard transformed the operations workflow. With a single interface to monitor and manage shipments, the ops team was able to **handle twice the number of shipments without increasing headcount**, directly contributing to the startup’s growth targets. Data accuracy improved dramatically, cutting manual reconciliation time by 70 %. The modular architecture also positioned the company to add new features—such as automated carrier bidding and predictive delivery estimates—without major rewrites. Sara’s solution delivered measurable efficiency gains and laid a solid technical foundation for the startup’s scaling ambitions.
